Downstairs

A bookstore beneath your feet

The Blessed Fig Tree opened in downtown Antigo in May 2023, in the former Buckalows Eatery at 800 Fifth Avenue. It carries about 4,000 books, mostly new with some used: history, philosophy, fantasy, Wisconsin and outdoors titles, and a manga section the shop counts among the best in northern Wisconsin.

The owner runs the shop and is usually downstairs during business hours. Ask for a recommendation, grab an unusual snack, or pick out something to read upstairs.

What you will find downstairs

  • Lakes and streamsThe county counts 766 lakes and 391 miles of trout streams, plus groomed cross-country ski trails in winter.

A short history of Antigo

Antigo was founded in 1878 by Civil War veterans Francis Deleglise and George Eckart, and was incorporated in 1885. Timber built the early town. When the pine was cut, farmers turned to dairying, and organizations formed under county agricultural agent E.G. Swovadam helped make Antigo Wisconsin's leading producer of Italian cheese.

Around the turn of the 20th century the town became a transport center on the Chicago and Northwestern Railroad, and it is the seat of Langlade County. People still call it the Gateway to Wisconsin's Northwoods.

Two places keep that story close to downtown. The Deleglise family's 1878 cabin now stands on the museum grounds at 404 Superior St, and the museum itself opened in 1997 inside the Carnegie library that served Antigo from 1905, funded by a $15,000 grant from Andrew Carnegie.

Sources: Wisconsin Historical Society, Antigo Chamber of Commerce, Travel Wisconsin, Langlade County.

Explore Antigo

A working Northwoods town

Langlade County calls itself the County of Trails, and Antigo is an Ice Age Trail Community. You can spend a day on foot without moving the car.

  • DowntownDiners, pizza, antiques and independent shops within a few blocks.
  • The public libraryDirectly across the street from the building.
  • Springbrook TrailWalking and cycling paths, boardwalks and water views inside town.
  • Ice Age TrailMore than 50 miles of the national scenic trail run through Langlade County.
  • Local historyThe Langlade County Historical Society museum and seasonal events.
  • Forests and lakesHiking, fishing, mountain biking and snowshoeing nearby.
